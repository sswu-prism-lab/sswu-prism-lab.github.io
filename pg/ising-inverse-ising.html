The critical point Tc

In the 2D Ising model, spins order below Tc = 2J/ln(1+√2) ≈ 2.269 J. There the magnetization rises and the susceptibility χ = N(⟨M²⟩−⟨M⟩²)/T diverges — long-range correlations at the transition.

Inverse Ising = max-entropy

The least-structured distribution matching the measured means ⟨sᵢ⟩ and correlations ⟨sᵢsⱼ⟩ is a pairwise Ising model p(s) ∝ e^{Σ hᵢsᵢ + Σ Jᵢⱼsᵢsⱼ}. Here we invert it with the mean-field estimate Ĵ = −C⁻¹.

Reading brain connectivity

Binarized neural signals (spikes, thresholded LFP/BOLD) are exactly such multi-channel binary data. Inverse-Ising / max-entropy recovers functional couplings between regions — a PRISM interest in network modeling from data.
